Industrial Tank Telemetry Demo

Making tank telemetry programmable: API, CLI, MCP, and agent-ready workflows for industrial IoT.

A synthetic, inspectable concept demo for field-aware inventory, alarm, monitor-health, location, diagnostics, webhook, and runout-risk workflows.

Inspectable Product Surfaces

REST API

Summary, tanks, readings, alerts, locations, diagnostics, alarm config, call schedules, map points, webhook events, and agent policy.

CLI

Repeatable support and integration workflows through tankctl, including diagnostics and runout-risk review.

MCP

Read-only tools for agents to summarize inventory, inspect tank detail, explain delivery priority, and review monitor health.

Governance

Read-only defaults, explicit write boundaries, approval gates, and audit expectations for production workflows.

Field-Aware Model

InventoryNet/gross volume, level, capacity, volume percentage, available capacity, and reading history.
AlarmsLow/high thresholds, critical limits, alarm status, safety stock, reorder level, fill detect, and short-fill events.
Monitor HealthCommunication status, sensor status, battery percentage, RSSI, RTU diagnostics, call schedules, and failed calls.

Governed Programmability

Agents and integrations can read summaries, tank status, diagnostics, alerts, runout risk, location metadata, and field catalogs. Operational writes such as moving tanks, editing thresholds, acknowledging alerts, creating delivery orders, notifying customers, or changing users are intentionally excluded from this demo and should require explicit scopes, audit logs, and human approval in production.

Data Boundary

This demo uses synthetic data only. It does not access AMETEK, Telular, SkyBitz, SmartTank, customer systems, proprietary portals, screenshots, logos, customer names, street addresses, users, emails, phone numbers, real device IDs, or proprietary records. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by those companies.
